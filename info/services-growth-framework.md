## **Automation - Be Specific** 

Automation is not a buzzword. Every automation must show what happens before, what becomes automatic and what business result it is meant to support. 

###### **Google Review Reply Workflow** 

New review -> alert/workflow -> AI-assisted draft -> human approval where required -> reply -> unanswered reviews tracked. 

###### **Email Follow-Up Automation** 

New lead -> confirmation email -> assigned owner -> follow-up reminder -> next email/task -> CRM status updated. 

###### **Appointment Automation** 

Booking -> confirmation -> reminder -> staff notification -> post-meeting follow-up. 

###### **Lead Routing** 

New enquiry -> source captured -> correct employee assigned -> response timer starts -> manager sees overdue leads. 

###### **Customer Reactivation** 

Inactive customer segment -> relevant return offer -> campaign -> response tracked -> follow-up. 

###### **Reporting Dashboard** 

Sales/lead/activity data -> central dashboard -> weekly management review instead of manual spreadsheet chasing. 

###### **Internal Notifications** 

Important event or overdue task -> right employee notified automatically -> escalation if unresolved. 

###### **Feedback Workflow** 

Customer interaction -> feedback request -> responses categorised -> negative feedback routed internally -> positive experience can be invited to leave an honest public review without incentives. 

###### **Automation rule** 

We do not automate something simply because we can. Automation must save time, reduce mistakes, improve sales, improve customer experience, reduce costs or improve management control.

## **How We Work** 

The client should not need to diagnose themselves. Akin Group starts by understanding the business and finding the biggest economic problem before recommending a solution. 

###### **1. Understand** 

Learn how the business gets customers, sells, retains them and operates. 

###### **2. Diagnose** 

Identify the biggest revenue leak, bottleneck or waste. 

###### **3. Prioritise** 

Decide what should be fixed first and what should be left alone. 

###### **4. Plan** 

Agree the solution, scope, responsibility, timeline and price. 

###### **5. Implement** 

Set up the process, system, automation or customer journey. 

###### **6. Train** 

Make sure the team knows how to use the solution. 

###### **7. Measure** 

Compare the right indicators before and after where data is available. 

###### **8. Improve** 

Optimise what works and stop what does not. 

#### **The opening offer** 

###### **Business Growth Check-Up** 

A short diagnostic conversation that looks at customer acquisition, conversion, repeat business, operations and systems. The purpose is to find the 1-3 areas worth fixing first, not to force a package. 

##### **Questions the check-up should answer** 

- How are new customers currently found? 

- What happens after someone becomes a lead or enquiry? 

- Where are opportunities being forgotten or lost? 

- How does the business increase customer value? 

- What brings customers back? 

- How are old customers or leads reactivated? 

- How do happy customers create reviews or referrals? 

- What repetitive work is still manual? 

- What numbers does management actually track?

## **Akin Group Non-Negotiables** 

These rules protect the company from becoming a low-margin, random-service agency. 

**01  No new service without a clear customer problem. 02  No industry expansion until Hospitality and Education show repeatable demand.** 

**03  No technology build just because it is possible.** 

**04  No false revenue promises or invented case-study metrics.** 

**05** No fake experience or pretending to be a larger team. 

**06** No project without clear scope, price, owner and timeline. 

**07** No implementation work before agreed payment/deposit terms. 

**08** No unlimited revisions or open-ended custom work. 

**09** No automation without a measurable reason to automate. 

**10** No customer data collection without appropriate privacy, lawful-basis and security practices. 

**11** No Google-review incentives or fake-review practices. 

**12** No legal, tax or regulated advice outside actual qualifications; coordinate with specialists instead. 

**13** No case study published without client permission or proper anonymisation. 

**14** Every successful project should create proof: result, testimonial, referral or reusable learning.
