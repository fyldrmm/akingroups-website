How We Work

The client should not need to diagnose themselves. We start by understanding the business and finding the biggest economic problem before recommending a solution.

1. Understand

Learn how the business gets customers, sells, retains them and operates.

2. Diagnose

Identify the biggest revenue leak, bottleneck or waste.

3. Prioritise

Decide what should be fixed first and what should be left alone.

4. Plan

Agree the solution, scope, responsibility, timeline and price.

5. Implement

Set up the process, system, automation or customer journey.

6. Train

Make sure the team knows how to use the solution.

7. Measure

Compare the right indicators before and after where data is available.

8. Improve

Optimise what works and stop what does not.

The opening offer: Business Growth Check-Up

A short diagnostic conversation that looks at customer acquisition, conversion, repeat business, operations and systems. The purpose is to find the 1–3 areas worth fixing first, not to force a package.

Questions the check-up should answer

  • How are new customers currently found?
  • What happens after someone becomes a lead or enquiry?
  • Where are opportunities being forgotten or lost?
  • How does the business increase customer value?
  • What brings customers back?
  • How are old customers or leads reactivated?
  • How do happy customers create reviews or referrals?
  • What repetitive work is still manual?
  • What numbers does management actually track?
